One Lane Road

Each car gets a desired speed drawn from a Gaussian curve (mean 30 mph). Cars can't pass, so faster drivers get stuck behind slower ones. The road is folded into four rows of a quarter mile each; traffic flows left to right, then down. Ramp cars use a zipper merge: they never merge into an occupied spot, hold to the end of the acceleration lane when traffic is under 25 mph, and each mainline car lets in at most one ramp car, so the two streams alternate.
